## Folding the groups

Each group on the Dashboard (**Today**, **Pilot results**, **Clinic tools**,
**Live campaign activity**, **Recent management activity**, and **Platform**
for platform admins) folds and unfolds by clicking its title. A folded group
shows its key numbers on one line. The browser remembers what you folded.
**Clinic tools** (appointment slots, the clinic map, patient call history) and
the activity lists start folded.

## Today

The top of the **Dashboard** shows the day's work for the selected clinic:

- **Appointments today**, and how many the patient confirmed.
- **Still to come, not confirmed**: booked for later today with no reply to
  the reminder yet; a call may help.
- **Past visits to mark**: appointments in the last 14 days not yet marked.
  Click it for the list, then **Came** or **No-show** on each. This is what
  the no-show rate is counted from, so mark visits every day. Every change is
  logged.
- **CC action items** that are open (clinical items only for nurses and doctors).
- **Readings to review** (nurses and doctors), with how many are critical.

## Pilot results

Choose **Last 7 days** or **Last 30 days**. All numbers are counts for the clinic:

- **No-show rate**: no-shows out of the visits marked Came or No-show, compared
  with the clinic's **baseline** (the rate before CareCall) and the previous
  period. A clinic admin sets the baseline with **Change** under the cards.
- **Self sign-ups** (from the sign-up poster) and all new patients.
- **Connected to chat** in the period, and how many of the clinic's patients
  are on chat in total.
- **Appointments booked**, by where they came from (staff, symptom check,
  booking link, AI call).
- **Reminders sent** and the replies: confirmed, moved, cancelled.

Nurses and doctors also see **Care plans and notes**: medicine adherence (doses
taken out of those due), readings sent back out of those requested, readings
outside target and critical, monitoring plans running, and notes signed (also
per doctor per week).

## Answers to common questions

- "Why is the no-show rate blank?" No visits were marked Came or No-show in
  the period. Use **Past visits to mark**.
- "Why can't I see care numbers?" They are for nurses and doctors only.
- CC can explain any of these numbers; for campaign results ask CC or open
  the campaign.
