This is general information, not legal advice. Clinics should confirm their
obligations with their Data Protection Officer or counsel.

- Health information is sensitive personal information under the Data Privacy
  Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Collect consent for reminders and keep a record.
- **SMS consent** is a per-patient flag set on import or edit.
- **Chat app consent** is recorded when the patient replies YES in the app.
  STOP is honoured immediately.
- Messages and voicemails never include diagnoses or health details, only the
  clinic name, a callback number and, where enabled, a booking link.
- Booking links store only a hash of the token. Patients must confirm their
  date of birth before seeing or choosing times.
- Staff only see their own clinic's data.
- CC sees only aggregate numbers, not names or phone numbers.
