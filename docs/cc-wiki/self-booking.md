When self-booking is enabled for the clinic, messages to patients can include a
private booking link. The patient verifies their date of birth on the page,
then picks a day and time from the same availability the AI offers.

- Links last 7 days. Each patient has at most one active link per campaign.
- Booking through the link marks the patient booked and cancels the pending call.
- Too many wrong DOB attempts lock the link and send the patient for human follow-up.
- On chat apps, patients can reply **BOOK** to receive a fresh link.

Self-booking is switched on per clinic by a platform admin. There is no toggle
in Clinic settings yet.
