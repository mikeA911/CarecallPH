## Steps

1. **Invite patients.** Open a patient on **Patients** and click **Invite to
   chat**. Send the English or Filipino message from the clinic's phone, or let
   the patient scan the QR code at the front desk. The patient picks an app,
   replies with their **date of birth**, then **YES**.
2. **Check it worked.** The patient's **Chat apps** table shows **Active** once
   they've said YES.
3. **Create a chat campaign.** On **New campaign**, choose **Chat message with a
   booking link** for How to reach patients, tick the patients and save.
4. **Start sending.** On the campaign page, click **Start sending**. Messages go
   out inside calling hours. Invite anyone listed as **needs chat invite**.
5. **Optional, once SMS is set up:** in Clinic settings turn on **Chat app
   invites in text messages** so call campaigns invite patients too.
6. After a few days, ask CC how many patients booked and how many messages went
   out per app.

A clinic-wide front-desk QR code (not tied to one patient) is planned.
