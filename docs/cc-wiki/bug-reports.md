## Reporting a problem

Open CC and say what went wrong, or tap **Report a problem**. CC:

1. checks whether it's how CareCall is meant to work (and says so if it is);
2. asks what happened (any error message word for word), what you expected,
   and the steps to make it happen again, if you know them;
3. asks how bad it is: **blocker** (you can't do your work), **problem**
   (wrong, but there's a way around it) or **annoyance**;
4. reads a short summary back and files it only when you say yes;
5. gives you the report number.

The screen you were on and your clinic are added automatically. **Never put
patient names, phone numbers or birthdays in a report**: say "a patient"
instead. A report with a phone number in it is refused.

## Checking on a report

Ask CC "what happened to my bug reports?" It lists your reports with their
number and status: **New**, **Seen**, **Fixed** or **Won't fix**, plus any
note from the CareCall team. You only see your own reports.

## Bug reports screen (platform admins)

**Bug reports** under Platform lists every report, new ones first, then by
how bad they are. Click a report to see what happened, what was expected and
the steps, set the status and add a note for the reporter. Only the status and
note can change; what the user reported stays as filed.
