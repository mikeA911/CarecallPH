## Reporting a problem

Open CC and say what went wrong, or tap **Report a problem**. CC:

1. checks whether it's how CareCall is meant to work (and says so if it is);
2. asks what happened (any error message word for word), what you expected,
   and the steps to make it happen again, if you know them;
3. asks how bad it is: **blocker** (you can't do your work), **problem**
   (wrong, but there's a way around it) or **annoyance**;
4. reads a short summary back and files it only when you say yes;
5. gives you the report number.

The screen you were on, your clinic and the page's recent errors and failed
requests (which part of CareCall failed and the error code; never patient
data) are added automatically. **Never put patient names, phone numbers or
birthdays in a report**: say "a patient" instead. A report with a phone number
in it is refused.

## Adding a screenshot

After filing, CC offers **Take screenshot** of the page behind the CC panel.
Patient details (names, numbers, dates, table cells, form fields) are blurred;
headings, buttons and error messages stay readable. You see a preview first
and nothing is sent until you tap **Attach** (or **No thanks**). Tick **Show
patient details** only if the problem is in the data itself: the CareCall
team will see them. Up to 3 screenshots per report, within an hour of filing.
Screenshots are deleted after 90 days.

## Checking on a report

Ask CC "what happened to my bug reports?" It lists your reports with their
number and status: **New**, **Seen**, **Fixed** or **Won't fix**, plus any
note from the CareCall team. You only see your own reports.

## Bug reports screen (platform admins)

**Bug reports** under Platform lists every report, new ones first, then by
how bad they are. Click a report to see what happened, what was expected and
the steps, the page's errors and failed requests, and any screenshots (marked
when patient details were shown), then set the status and add a note for the
reporter. Only the status and
note can change; what the user reported stays as filed.
